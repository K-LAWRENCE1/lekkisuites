How it works

Whoever books a room — online or at the desk — gets a real room number straight away, so that room turns booked and nobody else can be sold it for the same dates.

The room frees itself once its check-out date has passed. You never have to remember to unlock it. Tap a booked tile and the board tells you who holds it and until when.

locked is different: that is you pulling a room out by hand (maintenance, staff use, a guest not recorded yet). Only you can put it back.
