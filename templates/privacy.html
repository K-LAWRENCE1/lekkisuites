Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you give us

Lekki Suites · 1 Kayode Otitoju, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria · last updated November 2026

This policy explains what information Lekki Suites collects when you use this website, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. We only ask for what we need to hold your room and serve you.

1. Who we are

Lekki Suites is a hotel at 1 Kayode Otitoju, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria. For any question about this policy or your information, email {{ hotel_email }} or call {{ hotel_phone_display }}.

2. Information we collect

  • When you make a reservation: your name, phone number, email address, number of guests, the room type, your check-in and check-out dates, and any special requests you type into the form.
  • When you order food or drink: the name you give, your room number and your phone number.
  • When you check your booking ticket: the reference you enter, so we can show the booking that belongs to it.
  • Technical information: your browser sends basic request information (such as your IP address and the pages you open) to our hosting provider as part of serving the site.

We do not collect your card or bank details on this website. Payment is completed through the channel shown at checkout, and any card details you enter there are handled by that payment provider, not stored by us.

3. Why we use it

  • To confirm, hold and manage your reservation.
  • To contact you about your booking, your arrival or your food order.
  • To prepare your room and pass your requests to the right staff.
  • To keep booking and payment records for accounting and legal purposes.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use a small session cookie to remember your food cart between pages and, for staff, to keep an admin login active. We also store a flag on your device so the site knows you have already dismissed the “add to home screen” prompt. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

5. Who we share it with

  • Our staff, at the front desk and in the kitchen, so your booking or order can be served.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us — hosting, and our email and payment providers — only for that purpose.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it, or where we must register guests as required by Nigerian hospitality regulations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not pass it to anyone for marketing.

6. How long we keep it

Booking and order records are kept for as long as we need them for our accounts and for any legal or tax requirement. If you ask us to delete information we are not required to keep, we will do so.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can ask to see the information we hold about you.
  • You can ask us to correct anything that is wrong.
  • You can ask us to delete information we no longer need.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you for anything other than your booking.

Send any of these requests to {{ hotel_email }} and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Security

The site is served over a secure connection and access to booking records is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfect, so please do not send sensitive documents by ordinary email.

9. Children

This website is not intended for children to use on their own. Bookings should be made by an adult guest, who is responsible for the information given about everyone in the party.

10. Changes to this policy

If our practice changes, we will update this page and change the date at the top. The version shown here is the one that applies when you use the site.
