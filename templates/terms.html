Booking Policy

Terms of service for online reservations

Lekki Suites · 1 Kayode Otitoju, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria

Thank you for choosing Lekki Suites. We are committed to providing our guests with a comfortable, safe and enjoyable stay. To ensure a smooth booking and check-in experience, kindly read and understand our booking policies before making a reservation.

1. Reservation and Booking Confirmation

  • All reservations are subject to room availability.
  • A booking is only confirmed upon receipt of full payment.
  • We do not hold or reserve rooms without payment.
  • Guests are required to provide accurate personal information and valid identification details when making a reservation.
  • Booking confirmation will be communicated once payment has been verified.

2. Payment Policy

  • Full payment is required before check-in.
  • Guests are advised to confirm room availability and rates before making payment.
  • Any additional services or expenses incurred during the stay must be settled before checkout.
  • Proof of payment may be requested where necessary.

3. Check-in and Check-out Policy

  • Check-in: 1:00 PM
  • Check-out: 12:00 Noon
  • Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may attract additional charges.
  • Guests who check in before 7:00 AM must check out the same day.
  • Guests who wish to extend their stay must inform the front desk and make payment before the original check-out time.
  • Failure to check out at the stipulated time without prior approval may attract additional charges.

4. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • All bookings are non-refundable once payment has been made.
  • Cancellations or changes to reservations do not automatically qualify for a refund.
  • In exceptional circumstances, management may consider a one-time rescheduling, subject to approval and availability.
  • Guests are advised to confirm their travel plans before completing payment.

5. Booking Modification and Extension

  • Requests to change booking dates or room categories must be communicated to management in advance.
  • All modifications are subject to availability and applicable rate differences.
  • Room extensions must be approved and paid for before the current booking expires.

6. Guest Identification and Registration

  • All guests must present a valid means of identification upon arrival.
  • Visitors may be required to register at the reception.
  • Management reserves the right to refuse accommodation where proper identification or booking verification cannot be provided.

7. Guest Conduct and Hotel Rules

  • Guests are expected to maintain proper conduct and respect other guests and hotel staff.
  • Smoking is prohibited in designated non-smoking areas.
  • Illegal activities, violence, harassment and disruptive behaviour are strictly prohibited.
  • Guests are responsible for any damage to hotel property caused by themselves or their visitors.
  • Management reserves the right to terminate a stay without a refund where a guest seriously violates hotel rules.

8. Personal Belongings and Valuables

  • Guests are responsible for their personal belongings and valuables throughout their stay.
  • Lekki Suites will not be held responsible for personal items left unattended or lost due to negligence.

9. Right of Admission

Lekki Suites reserves the right to refuse admission or discontinue accommodation where a guest violates hotel policies, provides false booking information or engages in activities that compromise the safety and comfort of others.

10. Acceptance of Policy

By completing an online reservation and making payment, guests acknowledge that they have read, understood and agreed to abide by the terms and conditions outlined in this policy.

Thank you for choosing Lekki Suites. We look forward to welcoming you and making your stay comfortable and memorable.

Lekki Suites Management
Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria.
