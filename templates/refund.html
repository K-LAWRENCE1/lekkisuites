Refund & Cancellation

Cancellations, modifications and refunds

Lekki Suites · 1 Kayode Otitoju, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria

Please read our cancellation and refund terms carefully before completing payment for a reservation.

1. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • All bookings are non-refundable once payment has been made.
  • Cancellations or changes to reservations do not automatically qualify for a refund.
  • In exceptional circumstances, management may consider a one-time rescheduling, subject to approval and availability.
  • Guests are advised to confirm their travel plans before completing payment.

2. Booking Modification and Extension

  • Requests to change booking dates or room categories must be communicated to management in advance.
  • All modifications are subject to availability and applicable rate differences.
  • Room extensions must be approved and paid for before the current booking expires.

3. No-Show and Early Departure

  • If a guest does not arrive on the booked arrival date and does not contact the hotel, the booking is treated as a no-show and is not refundable.
  • Leaving before the booked check-out date does not create a refund for the unused nights.

4. When the Hotel Cannot Honour a Booking

If Lekki Suites is unable to provide the room that has been paid for — for example because of a maintenance fault, a double booking, or an event outside the hotel's control — management will contact the guest directly to arrange a suitable alternative room or a refund of the amount paid for the affected nights.

5. How Refunds Are Paid

  • Where a refund is approved, it is returned to the same card or account used for the payment. A refund cannot be sent to a different account.
  • Online payments are processed by Paystack, so the refund appears on your statement once your bank has cleared it. How long that takes is set by your bank, not by the hotel.
  • Payments made by bank transfer or cash at reception are refunded by the hotel directly.

6. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Contact the hotel on +234 809 229 9428 or reservations@lekkisuites.com, quoting your ticket number, the name on the booking, the dates involved and the reason for the request. The front desk will confirm in writing whether the request has been approved.

7. Related Policies

This page should be read together with our Booking Policy, which covers check-in and check-out times, identification, hotel rules and guest conduct.
